Controls

Moving

Move W A S D
Jump / swim Space
Sneak Shift (won't fall off edges)
Sprint double-tap W, or hold Ctrl
Fly (creative) double-tap Space
Camera F cycles first person / third person / front view

Mining & using

Mine / attack hold left mouse · hit while falling for a critical hit
Place / use / eat right mouse
Powers menu P
Laser Eyes (superpower) hold L
Guns hold right mouse to aim (Sniper: scope), left mouse to fire (SMG: hold = auto)
Throw grenade right mouse
Armor right-click to wear · click the armor slots in your inventory to swap
Bed right-click to set your respawn point · right-click at night to sleep
Door right-click to place (needs 2 blocks), right-click to open/close

Inventory

Hotbar 1–9 or the scroll wheel
Inventory E
Drop Q drops one · with Ctrl held, the stack
Sort inventory R (with the inventory open)
Clicks click = pick up/place a stack · right-click = half/one · shift-click = quick move · shift-click a result = craft all · Recipes (Crafting Table) fills in a recipe
Dragging drag left to share a stack out · drag right to place one each · double-click to gather · shift-drag to quick-move
Over a slot the wheel moves one item · 1–9 swaps with the hotbar · Q throws · click outside the window to throw
Creative middle click gives a full stack
Chest label click the small slot beside a chest window's ✕ with an item to show it on the chest's sides (the item stays yours) · click it empty-handed, or right-click, to clear

Vehicles & mounts

Drive car right-click a car · W/S accelerate · A/D steer · Shift gets out · H honks
Boat right-click water to launch · W/S throttle · A/D steer · Shift hops out
Plane right-click to board · Space throttle up · Alt brake and throttle down (stopped on the ground: backs up) · the throttle stays where you leave it (the bar on the right) · W nose down · S nose up · A/D turn · Shift gets out · where you look does not steer
Skateboard right-click to ride · W push · A/D carve · Space ollie · A/D in the air spins · land on a block edge to grind · Shift steps off
Coaster cart right-click to hop in · hold W to roll out of the station
Dragons feed 5 steak to tame · right-click bare-handed to ride · fly where you look, Space climbs, Shift dismounts

Special items

Player Compass right-click to cycle players (sneak = back)
Critter Jar right-click to throw — a hit captures the mob, throw the filled jar to release it
Structure Compass right-click to tune it to a structure
Gadgets active gadgets fire on right-click while held
Subscribe Button right-click to throw
Disaster Sphere right-click to choose and unleash a disaster nearby (one use)
Aliens right-click a villager to talk · Esc closes the conversation

Screen & chat

Chat T or Enter to talk · / for commands · /msg <player> to whisper · /help
Player list hold Tab
Screenshot F2 saves a picture (PNG)
Save a replay F8 saves the last 30 seconds as a video (Replays: turn on/off in Settings)
Debug F3 · F3 + B hitboxes · F3 + G chunk borders
Pause Esc

Creativity & mini-games

Creativity Easel: right-click to paint (B pencil · E eraser · G fill · I picker · right-click erases · Ctrl+Z undo; Esc keeps your work) · Done gives the painting: right-click a wall to hang it · Record player: click squares for notes, Space plays, Done presses the disc · Jukebox: right-click with a disc to play it, again to eject
DEEP DIRT 2D right-click a placed Gaming Rig to play · WASD/Space move · left mouse mine/fight · right mouse build · 1-6 select · C craft · Esc saves and quits (its own keys: they do not change)

Touch

Touch left stick = move · drag the right side = look · quick tap = place/use · press & hold = mine/attack · JUMP double-tap = fly (creative)

Change keys

Click a key, then press the one you want (mouse buttons 4 and 5 work too). Esc cancels. A key already in use swaps places. Left, right and middle click, Esc, Enter and F3 stay as they are. Saved in this browser.
